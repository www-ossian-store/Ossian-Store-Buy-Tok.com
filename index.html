1. Pay with Account Balance
Instant Credit
How to Transfer:
  1. Sign in to your OSSIAN PRO account and select any of the 20 Token Packages.
  2. Select 1. Pay with Account Balance in the checkout popup.
  3. Verify your current USD Balance covers the package cost, then tap Pay Instantly from Account Balance.
  4. No Transaction ID or manual form is required — tokens are credited immediately.
Rules:
  • Your Account Balance must be equal to or greater than the package price.
  • Once purchased via Account Balance, the transaction is final and non-refundable.
2. Binance Pay (Send by UID)
Manual Verification
How to Transfer:
  1. Select 2. Binance Pay at checkout and copy the official company Binance UID shown.
  2. Open your Binance App → Go to Pay → Tap Send → Choose Binance ID (UID).
  3. Paste the company UID, enter the exact USDT/USD package amount, and confirm payment.
  4. In checkout, paste only your Binance Pay Order ID and your Sender Binance UID, then submit.
Rules:
  • Always send the exact package amount — underpaid orders will be rejected.
  • Never reuse an old Order ID or submit someone else's Binance Pay receipt.
  • Verification completes shortly after submission; tokens are credited automatically once approved.
